## The brief

When you think of Christmas, you don't think of Afterpay. The campaign's job was
to change that — to make Afterpay the way people find and buy gifts, and to
frame it as financial flexibility over a cost-heavy period rather than another
trap.

## The execution

There's no one way to Christmas. Real tree die-hard or proud member of the
#faketreefam, deck-the-halls or stick-a-bauble-on-a-houseplant-and-call-it-a-tree
— the platform held every version of the day under one line, and let the retail
network prove it.

## What I wrote

One of a team of three that created the concept and the platform line,
“However you Christmas, Afterpay it.” Ideated the innovation activations — Merry Re-giftmas and its re-gifting pool mechanic, the
drop-a-hint-and-win catalogue, the AI Christmas memory generator.
