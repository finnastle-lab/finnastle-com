## The brief

Margaret River's first craft spirit distillery, moving from family operation to
something with reach. It needed brand positioning, packaging copy and campaign
headlines that could carry the distillery, the accommodation and the product
range without the thread going slack. It also needed to launch the Collector's
Series, a run of limited-edition bottles drawn from the distillery's own
history, on no media budget.

## The execution

The Spirit Of does double duty — the liquor and the place, the same two words.
Underneath it the territory splits by job: *Distilled with a story. Delivered to
you.* carries the direct-to-consumer promise, and *Made on this land. Distilled
in history.* carries the provenance.

Hot Cross Rum opened the Collector's Series. Four-year barrel-aged dark rum
steeped with raisins, orange, cinnamon, cloves and vanilla, 40% ABV, bottled in
500ml French cubic glass with a hand-numbered calligraphy label on each. The
scarcity was the mechanic and the story. Two hundred bottles, sold direct, timed
to Easter, with no media spend behind any of it.

## What I wrote

The platform and its supporting lines, plus the product campaign headlines.
Unlike Any Other, for The Spiced Rum. Made in Margaret River.

The Hot Cross Rum media releases and key messaging. Hot Cross Rum: cheers to an
unforgettable Easter. Infused with Easter cellar-bration. Sugar, spice,
everything nice. Holy, Spirit! You can own a piece of Australia's distilling
history. The rum came back every Easter after that. Concrete Playground covered
the fourth run nationally in March 2023, 120 hand-numbered bottles at $110, with
labels by Perth artist Stacey Teo.
