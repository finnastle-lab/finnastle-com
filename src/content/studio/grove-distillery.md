## The brief

Margaret River's first craft spirit distillery, moving from family operation to
something with reach. It needed brand positioning, packaging copy and campaign
headlines that could carry the distillery, the accommodation and the product
range.

## The execution

The Spirit Of. *Distilled with a story. Delivered to you.* *Made on this land.
Distilled in history.*

## What I wrote

The platform and its supporting lines, plus the product campaign headlines.
Unlike Any Other, for The Spiced Rum. Made in Margaret River.
