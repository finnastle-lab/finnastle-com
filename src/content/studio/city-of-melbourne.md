## The brief

Nobody reads council guidance for pleasure. They arrive at it because they have
a fine, a ruling or a deadline, and they want one answer fast. City of
Melbourne's public-facing guidance on fines, rulings and the detail attached to
them had to work for people already on the back foot — which makes the
architecture the message.

## The execution

I was embedded in the City of Melbourne offices on behalf of VERSA, as the
conversational flow expert on the team. The work ran across the information
architecture, the email flow and the website content as one system: what a
person needs to know, in what order, and what the next step is at every point
they might stop. Treating the guidance as a conversation rather than a document
set is what keeps someone moving instead of calling to ask.
