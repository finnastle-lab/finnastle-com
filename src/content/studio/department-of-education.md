## The brief

One department, two audiences who share almost nothing. Teachers need to run
their working day; students need to learn and to be told what's changing.
Building a series of sites across both meant the content had to hold the same
institutional voice while the register moved with the reader.

## The execution

A whole series of websites for the Department of Education under Deepend and
VERSA: a learning portal, the business-as-usual organisational sites, and the
public school updates. Teachers and students alike, which in practice means the
same information structured twice — one version for the person delivering it and
one for the person on the receiving end.

## What I wrote

Content strategy across the series, covering the learning portal, the BAU
organisational sites and the public school updates.
