## The brief

Tekspace sells trust in a market built on scepticism. Cyber security buyers are
burned out on vendor marketing — every product claims to be the best, and IT
leaders have no independent way to check. The team had operator-level expertise
to say something real and had never made it public. No research asset, no earned
media, and a small marketing function competing for attention against far
better-resourced vendors.

## The execution

I designed and ran the Tekspace Cyber Lab, an in-house research program that
turns operator experience into independent, data-backed vendor analysis. It
benchmarks every major vendor in a category against a proprietary scoring
framework, grading real capabilities rather than marketing claims. Three reports
shipped across two sprints: email security across 18 vendors, then browser
security across 13 and security awareness training across 20 — 400+ raw features
normalised into 40 comparable capabilities in one report alone.

## What I wrote

The research program and the reports, plus an agentic AI production pipeline
built from scratch that doubled output sprint-over-sprint on no extra budget or
headcount. The work was cited in Google's AI Overview as a primary source for
browser-security-vendor queries, and picked up by TechDay Australia,
SecurityBrief Australia, ChannelLife AU and IT Brief.
