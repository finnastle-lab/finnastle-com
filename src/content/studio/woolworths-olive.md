## The brief

Olive was the Woolworths customer chatbot. The source material was the existing
longform FAQ — accurate, exhaustive, and written for a page rather than for
somebody mid-question in a chat window.

## The execution

Editing the FAQ library down into chatbot content, taking the project from
design through to content. My first chatbot project, working under Tyler's
leadership.

## What I wrote

Conversational design and writing for Olive, as a writer with VERSA delivering
for WooliesX.
