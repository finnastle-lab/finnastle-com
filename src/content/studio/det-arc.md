## The brief

One department, two audiences who share almost nothing. Teachers need to run
their working day; students need to learn and to be told what's changing.
Building a series of sites across both meant the content had to hold the same
institutional voice while the register moved with the reader.

## The execution

A whole series of websites for the state Department of Education and Training
under Deepend and VERSA. ARC — access, resource, collaborate — is the learning
portal, and the name is the architecture: get to the thing, use the thing, work
on it with someone else. Alongside it sat the business-as-usual organisational
sites and the public school updates. Teachers and students alike, which in
practice means the same information structured twice: one version for the
person delivering it and one for the person on the receiving end.

Content strategy across the series, covering ARC, the BAU organisational sites
