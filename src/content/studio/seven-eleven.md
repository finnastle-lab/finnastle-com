## The brief

Twenty-five years of an American brand in Australia. The creative answer was to
celebrate the anniversary when Australia gets the most out of it. 7-Eleven is
open 24/7, so you can summer 24/7 — midnight ice creams to mid-morning coffees,
10am sunscreen or 10pm Slurpee.

## The execution

A looping brand film that takes the viewer from day to night and back again,
with short social edits that looped the same way. To handle the time-of-day
variables we shot each social edit twice, day and night, replicating frames and
keeping talent aligned across both versions. That made the edit switch utterly
seamless.

## What I wrote

The day-into-night transition scripts and the social-first copy for short
vertical formats, tuned to the morning-coffee-to-midnight-snack habit. We used
time variables to earmark edits for day and night placements.
