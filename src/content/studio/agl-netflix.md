## The brief

The modern home, where Netflix matters more than heating. Streaming is as common
as electricity now, and every house has shows it can't live without. The job was
a retail bundle that didn't feel like a bundle — a satirical campaign about
life's necessities.

## The execution

Energy paired with entertainment, built on the household friction everyone
recognises. If you can't live without Bridgerton, it's included in your
electricity plan. The line flexed into context without losing its shape: can't
live without Netflix while working out, while in the garden. Rolled out across
broadcast and tactical, price-based retail.

## What I wrote

The 15s and 30s broadcast spots, including the Bridgerton hook, and the brand
voice rollout across retail assets. Total dad energy.
