## The brief

The modern home, where Netflix matters more than heating. Streaming is as common
as electricity now, and every house has shows it can't live without. A satirical
campaign about life's necessities.

## The execution

Energy paired with entertainment. If you can't live without Bridgerton, it's
included in your electricity plan. Can't live without Netflix while working out?
While in the garden? Rolled out across broadcast and tactical, price-based
retail.

## What I wrote

The 15s and 30s broadcast spots, including the Bridgerton hook, and the brand
voice rollout across retail assets. Total dad energy.
