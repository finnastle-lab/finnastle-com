## The brief

Find a brand essence that could represent the product fearlessly. 96% of
children and 90% of adults don't get their recommended intake of fresh fruit or
veg, so the strategy was transparency from the packaging to the mission: make
sure fruit and veg gets eaten, not argued over or wasted.

## The execution

Two jobs, one platform. The identity had to talk to kids and to the adults
buying for them, which meant two tones of voice carrying the same proposition.
5 a day the easy way holds up on a lunchbox and on a shelf, so the ranges could
differentiate without splitting into two brands.

Then the packaging, where transparency stopped being a strategy word and became
a design decision. The ingredients are the hero of the pack. & nothing else.
Nothing proves a commitment to transparent nutrition like having nothing to hide
behind. Freeze-dried for maximum freshness, sealing in flavour and nutrition.
Rip, tip, shake, sip.

## What I wrote

Brand naming, both tones of voice, the packaging line and web copy across the
two ranges. & nothing else — the device that let the ingredients list carry the
front of pack on its own. Welly isn't just your 5 a day the easy way; the
mission is doing good for you while doing good for the planet. We think it's BS
that nutritionally great fruit and veg goes to landfill. Happy Apple, Mango
Passion, Tropicool Punch, Chocberry Shake, Berry Beets.
