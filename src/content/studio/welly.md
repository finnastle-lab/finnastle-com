## The brief

Find a brand essence that could represent the product fearlessly. 96% of
children and 90% of adults don't get their recommended intake of fresh fruit or
veg, so the strategy was transparency from the packaging to the mission: make
sure fruit and veg gets eaten, not argued over or wasted.

## The execution

If you're confident, less is more. 100% plants, nothing else — a single-minded
proposition that didn't hide the minimalism, and that let the kids' and adults'
packaging differentiate without two separate stories. Freeze-dried for maximum
freshness, sealing in flavour and nutrition. Rip, tip, shake, sip.

## What I wrote

Brand naming, tone of voice, packaging copy and web copy across both ranges.
Welly isn't just your 5 a day the easy way — the mission is doing good for you
while doing good for the planet. We think it's BS that nutritionally great fruit
and veg goes to landfill. Happy Apple, Mango Passion, Tropicool Punch, Chocberry
Shake, Berry Beets.
