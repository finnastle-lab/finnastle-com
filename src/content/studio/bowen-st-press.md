## The brief

Bowen Street Press is RMIT's publishing imprint. The work was the identity and
the publication design around *Rabbit 39: Mutiny*, a journal of nonfiction
poetry — a category that resists the shelf it's supposed to sit on.

## The execution

A journal for nonfiction poetry could be considered its own kind of sustained
mutiny. A space and a mode through which to refuse the orders and conventions of
genre, to reject the navigational tools that separate books into neat categories
on bookstore shelves, and to dwell in a less comfortable literary space to see
what might emerge.

Editing and the brand and publication design across the imprint and the issue.
