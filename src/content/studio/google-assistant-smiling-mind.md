## The brief

Smiling Mind is a mindfulness and mental health platform. Putting it on Google
Assistant meant the whole experience arrived as speech — no screen to retreat
to, and no second chance at a first line.

## The execution

Greetings matter. They're our first point of contact with a world that hasn't
met us yet. In life, when someone you don't know says hello, the way you say hey
back reveals a lot about you. The work modelled dialogue and user flows in
Dialogflow, translating conversational requirements into something that
functioned as a product and still sounded like a person.

## What I wrote

Scripted and built voice and chatbot experiences across Smiling Mind and, in the
same period, the Australian Red Cross, the City of Melbourne and the City of
Sydney.
