## The brief

Blue Space is a wellbeing platform for current and former Victoria Police
employees and their families — mental health information, resilience tools,
recovery stories and crisis support in one place. People whose job is protecting
the public have to protect themselves too, and the platform had to say that
without sounding like a policy document.

## The execution

Made for police, by police, in collaboration with thousands of people across the
Victoria Police community. The content had to carry crisis support and everyday
wellbeing in the same structure, cover help for yourself and help for someone
you love, and put privacy first at every step — because the thing standing
between someone and the resource is usually the worry that asking leaves a
record.

## What I wrote

Content strategy across the platform: the structure that has to carry crisis
support and everyday wellbeing at once, and the words that get someone from a
hard moment to the right resource. Blue Space is still running today, hosted
through Police Care Australia.
