## The brief

An integrated campaign and brand platform to help parents pick up after COVID.
If drop-off guilt is real, what makes it worth it? A story told in tantrums,
campaigning for early education by uniting parents through the drop-off drama.

## The execution

The Film Diary. A national talent search found parents who matched the vision,
and production began and ended with sifting through self-filmed drop-offs to
find the gold. Finding the sublime in the mundane — the moments where drop-off
tears feel worth it. A smile in the backseat, a handhold on the bus.

## What I wrote

The platform line and the copy framework around those small moments. Early
education is an important choice for parents who work, who want their kids to
learn, or who need the headspace. You're not leaving your kids at daycare.
You're loving them with us.
