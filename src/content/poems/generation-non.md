Slow days
slow day
slow day
pave the way
crumble away
fog in my eyes
morning dew
i left the bed
i left you
(sound)
salad days
dirty ways
get to work
8hrs
get paid
wish it fulfilled me the same way
Do you lay in bed and think at night about why
you lie in bed and think at night?
your past visits
an unwanted visitor
your future watches
the audience in a surgeons theatre
