Mungabareena reserve
Where the Murray river cuts through Victoria and New South Wales like an arc
Or a bloodline
Where pop and nan pic knocked
Mum and uncles skinny dipped
Sons took first dips in water
Like a baptism
Belonging
Float downstream
From Albury to wodonga
The water a bond
Shared by land and people
Like family and blood
