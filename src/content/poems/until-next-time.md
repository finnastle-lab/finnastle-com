A beginning always has
begin
for a first time
next time
will be the last time
so I jump
fall fast
passing you by
until next time
the waves met highways
& cars passed by
the yarra washes up
against blue skies
we met on the bridge for the first time
high
and clouds hang
low
now rain drops
trickles down
the lines of your neck
like track marks
to your heart
so
you jump
I follow
until next time
till next the time, minute, hour, day, year
we’re in this same place again
death he says
the boy
with a septum
snakes twist
along his arms
the cobra
hands me a beer
“ I don’t drink “
Says the voice
in my head.
I sip
and you jump
forward
I’m stuck on the bridge
between you and high
the bus driver has the number
of the bus we are on
written on the back of his hand
take a seat
