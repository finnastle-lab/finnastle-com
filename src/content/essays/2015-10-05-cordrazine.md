Two pieces for Cordrazine, an indie rock band from Melbourne. Their debut album From Here to
Wherever went to number nine on the ARIA albums chart and was nominated for Breakthrough Artist
at the 1998 ARIA Awards. This was their comeback song.

I was picked because the lead singer had seen [It's our problem.](/writing/2014-09-09-its-our-problem)
and requested something in the same style, in order to propose to his fiancée, now his wife.

You See In Me is grey-lead. Soft tones, cute.

written for her. The second part of the single I got to animate too.

Hey Man inverts the drawing. A totally different aesthetic.
