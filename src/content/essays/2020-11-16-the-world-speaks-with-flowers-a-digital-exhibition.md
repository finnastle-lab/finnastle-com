Floriography (The Langauge of Flowers)

There’s a word for speaking with Flowers, it’s called floriography. Until yesterday I had no idea what that word meant, but the word has helped me find meaning in what I do.

Which is quite simply draw flowers.

Flowers have taught me to see the beauty beyond their final flourish, they are just as much an extension of a plant as the stem, leaf or roots. We learn to watch flowers for the final moments, but really the flower is no more the beginning as it is the end of a flower’s lifecycle.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*fft8N1UrSU8cjNEXhz-6tQ.gif)
Flowers grow differently based on their environment

All flowers grow differently, and what flowers mean to us all is personal.

I’ve started to collect what they mean to me and my network here, to begin a digital dictionary of flowers, re-contextualizing the beauty for the future.

Flowers have evolved differently to look different over time, I wonder do they evolve to our idea of attractiveness?

Is there an intern or a purpose behind the beauty we see in flowers?

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*de2ZQYkJrFZJ9d2KjSibkg.png)
