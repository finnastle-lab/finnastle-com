## What this settles

The brief's first open question was which eight are actually held, because the hero image
and the article copy disagreed on two. The hero was right and the copy was wrong:

| Article said | Certificate says |
|---|---|
| Generative AI for Everyone | **AI for Research and Insights** |
| AI for Product Management | **AI for App Deployment** |

Both corrections are applied to `src/content/essays/four-things-i-built.md`. The article
keeps its own running order and reads as a sentence, not as a transcript of the certificate;
the set of eight now matches.
