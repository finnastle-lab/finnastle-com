GPT deliberately refused to pick between the two candidate pairs and left
`[CONFIRM CERTIFICATE 2]` and `[CONFIRM CERTIFICATE 8]`, on the grounds that six titles

> Eight certificates, for the record: AI Fundamentals, AI for Research and Insights, AI for Writing and Communicating, AI for Content Creation, AI for Brainstorming and Planning, AI for Data Analysis, AI for App Building, AI for App Deployment.

*AI for Product Management* → *AI for App Deployment*. The article's own running order is
kept, so it still reads as a sentence rather than a transcript of the certificate.

## 2. Name what is next — not applied, needs one fact

Replace `On to the next course.` with:

> Next is Google Cloud’s Generative AI Leader learning path. After that, I want to work towards its Professional Cloud Architect certification. Different credentials. The second asks a different question: what does this thing cost to run, and who does it fall over on?

That is the future-facing version, and it is the one in `revised-article-draft.md` because it
claims nothing that cannot be verified. **If Generative AI Leader study has already started**,
replace its first sentence with:

> I’ve already started Google Cloud’s Generative AI Leader learning path.

The original request for this work described the Google Cloud study as *ongoing*, which points
at the second variant — but "ongoing" in a brief is not a completion record either, and the
two variants make different claims about today. Pick one knowingly.

Do not claim the certification is held because the learning path is underway or finished.
They are separate things, and Generative AI Leader is not a prerequisite for Professional
Cloud Architect.
