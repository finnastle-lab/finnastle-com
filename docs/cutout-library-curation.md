# Cutout library curation — 23 October 2026

## Editorial rule

Keep identifiable subjects, personal drawing language and specific cultural references. Remove anonymous decoration and material too weak or composition-dependent to work as a reusable cutout. Age is not a default treatment.

## Sources and preservation

Figma Cutouts is the editorial baseline. Its existing names are not trusted as descriptions: e.g. the supposed horse group contains a reclining figure. Original nodes were not changed. Native isolated PNG renders are stored in `src/assets/cutouts`; source IDs and master hashes are in the manifest. WebPs resize proportionally to at most 1024 px. No recolouring, generated substitutes, background removal or redrawing was performed.

The catalogue is `public/icons/index.json`: gallery and flat compatibility listing share it. Different formats alone do not create variants. Full scenes remain separate from isolated pieces. Shared files under `src/assets/elements` are retained for articles but do not automatically enter the pack.
